We make materials that stop heat between battery cells

AeroTherm is a materials company that researches and develops nano-silica boards made with aerogel.

Our first product is an insulation board that goes between the cells of EV and ESS batteries, designed so that when one cell goes into thermal runaway, its heat does not spread to the cells beside it. Stopping propagation between cells also slows its spread to the module and ESS container level.

An aerogel board that also cuts radiant heat between cells

Heat moves by conduction, convection and radiation. The fine pores of the AeroTherm board cut conduction and convection through the air, and an infrared-shielding additive cuts the radiant heat that rises at thermal-runaway temperatures.

Blocking radiant heat

Core function

Radiant heat rises sharply with temperature (with the fourth power of absolute temperature). The infrared-shielding additive mixed into the board scatters and absorbs infrared, so radiant heat cannot pass straight through.

Fine pores and the Knudsen effect

The pores in the board are smaller than the mean free path of air molecules (about 70 nm at room temperature and atmospheric pressure). Molecules hit the pore walls instead of each other, which sharply cuts heat conduction through the gas, and the pores are too small for convection to develop.

Low solid conduction

Silica (SiO2) conducts heat poorly, and its thin skeleton is linked like a net, so heat moving through the solid has to follow long, narrow paths.
